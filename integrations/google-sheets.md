# Google Sheets Integration

Send [Formboost](https://formboost.app/) form submissions into Google Sheets for simple lead tracking, exports, and lightweight workflows.

## Typical flow

```text
Website form -> Formboost -> Google Sheets
```

## Setup

1. Open your form in the [Formboost dashboard](https://dashboard.formboost.app/).
2. Open **Integrations** and choose **Google Sheets**.
3. Connect the Google account and sheet requested by Formboost.
4. Map the submission fields if prompted.
5. Save the integration.
6. Submit a test form and verify a new row appears.

Your website still posts directly to:

```text
https://formboost.app/f/YOUR_ENDPOINT_ID
```

For current setup details, see the [official Formboost documentation](https://formboost.app/docs).
